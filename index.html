Game Rules & Guide

1

Dynamic Random Range

Every single round, the PC randomly chooses a fresh minimum and maximum number. The secret number is always locked inside this range.

2

Decipher The Feedback

If your guess is higher than the secret number, the PC alerts: "Too High! هبط شوية 🔽".

If your guess is lower, the PC alerts: "Too Low! طلع شوية 🔼".

3

Victory Condition

Match the secret number in the fewest possible attempts to score an SSS Rank! The PC reveals its secret number upon defeat.

4

Interactive Bound Tracking

Watch the narrowing search bar to visualize remaining possibilities and optimize your binary search strategy.
